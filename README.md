# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI Project

## 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive data analytics project developed using Power BI to analyze employee data and understand workforce trends, employee attrition, salary distribution, experience, and performance.

The project helps HR teams identify important patterns in employee turnover and make data-driven decisions to improve employee retention and workforce management.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze total employees and employee attrition.
* Calculate the employee attrition rate.
* Understand workforce distribution across departments.
* Identify job roles and departments with higher attrition.
* Analyze attrition based on age, salary, gender, education, and overtime.
* Understand employee experience, job satisfaction, and work-life balance.
* Create interactive dashboards to support HR decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

* **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
* **Microsoft Excel** – Data preparation and cleaning
* **Power Query** – Data transformation

## 🔍 Key Insights

1. The organization has 1,470 employees, of whom 237 have left.
2. The overall employee attrition rate is approximately 16.12%.
3. The Research & Development department has the highest number of employees.
4. Sales has a higher attrition rate than the other departments.
5. Laboratory Technicians and Sales Executives are among the job roles with the highest attrition counts.
6. Single employees account for the highest attrition count among the marital-status groups.
7. Employees working overtime have a higher observed attrition rate than employees not working overtime.
8. Job satisfaction and work-life balance can be explored to understand employee attrition patterns.
9. The dashboard helps compare workforce distribution across departments and employee groups.
10. Interactive filters make it easier to explore HR data and identify areas that may need attention.

*Note: These insights describe patterns in the dataset and do not establish the causes of employee attrition.*
